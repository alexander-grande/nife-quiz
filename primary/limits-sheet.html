  2. With PMU OFF, permissible maximum NP is [[n2]] +/- [[n2-tol]] %.
  3. Permissible at any operating condition “power setting” for completion of an in-flight emergency.
  4. Avoid stabilized ground operation from [[n4]] % NP.
  5. Operation in this range permitted only during aerobatics or spins, and [[n5-psi]] psi for [[n5-sec]] seconds with PCL at IDLE.
  6. Normal oil pressure during steady state conditions is [[n6-normal]] psi. Operation at oil pressure less than [[n6-low]] psi at flight idle or above is indicative of oil system malfunction.
  7. Acceptable for ground operation at and below [[n7]] torque.
  8. The PMU will govern maximum torque at [[n8-gov]] +/- [[n8-gov-tol]] %. Torque above [[n8-malf]] % at a constant PCL setting and steady flight is indicative of a governing system malfunction.
