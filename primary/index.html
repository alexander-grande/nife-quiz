EP Sheet

All twenty EPs, fillable

Laid out like the key. Write each step on its line, then check the whole sheet at once. The “IF…” decision lines are given.
⚠

EP Drill

One procedure, verbatim

A random EP title. Type the whole procedure from memory, in order, with the decision lines. Graded step by step.

Graded on the words that matter — “EMER LDG GR handle” and “emergency landing gear handle” are both right, and units are optional. Numbers must be exact, and steps are graded in order.

Operating Limits

Page 5 of the key — engine operating limits table and notes, airspeed, starter cycle, flight maneuvering, acceleration, and other limitations.

✎

Limits Sheet

Every blank on page 5, fillable

Laid out like the key. Fill in the table, notes, and limitations, then check the whole sheet at once.
⏱

Limits Drill

One limit at a time

A shuffled deck of every blank, asked cold with the units given. Retry the ones you missed until they stick.
